THE FORGOTTEN RUIN

Beneath the collapsed archways, where moss has claimed the old stone and torchlight has not burned in a hundred years, this scroll waited — bound in leather, sealed in wax, untouched by looters or time.

Those who broke the seal spoke of cold air rising from the floor, and of markings on the walls that were not there before.

Whatever this place once was, it does not welcome visitors. It only remembers them.

— sealed in shadow, bound in dust —
